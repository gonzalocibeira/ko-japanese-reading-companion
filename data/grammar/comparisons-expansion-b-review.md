## Author review

- Concession contrasts an actual delayed journey with a hypothetical reassurance. It explicitly allows factual ても in other contexts and does not characterize のに as inherently blaming. Highlighted both the changed subordinate ending and the complete changed main predicate.
- Inference grounds each choice in a specific scene without assigning rigid evidence sources or fixed confidence percentages. Noun attachment changes are complete: 休みらしい / 休みのようだ / 休みみたいだ.
- Qualified denial deliberately uses a case where both patterns are natural, explaining the different framing rather than inventing a forced translation contrast. The limit note distinguishes rejecting a proposed dislike motive from leaving dislike uncertain.
- Inclusion accepts both variants in the same difficult-question scene. It distinguishes extreme-example focus from extension of scope and does not impose an absolute strength ordering.
- Timing accepts both variants during the father’s outing. The distinction is opportunity before change versus an event within an interval; neither explanation falsely makes 間に imply an action lasting throughout the interval. Other gradual-change uses of うちに are explicitly outside this comparison.
- Inspected all Japanese with the actual bundled kuromoji tokenizer. Added `間に: あいだに` because the isolated highlighted span otherwise receives まに. The different phrase 間に合いました correctly remains まにあいました. No other contextual reading overrides were needed.
